QuizPliz

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 4, 2026 · Developer: Alisher Serikbayev

QuizPliz ("the App") is a fast, offline-first true/false trivia game. It does not require an account, a login, or any sign-up, and it does not ask you for any personal information. This policy explains what happens with your data — in short, almost nothing leaves your device.

1. Information stored on your device

Your gameplay data — scores, progress and tier, daily-challenge streak, and your chosen language — is stored locally on your device only (in the App's on-device database). We do not collect it, we cannot see it, and it is never transmitted to us or anyone else. Deleting the App removes this data.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. The App contains no analytics service, no crash-reporting service, and no third-party tracking. It works fully offline.

3. Advertising

This version of QuizPliz shows no ads and collects no advertising data. A future update may introduce an optional "watch an ad to continue" feature. If and when that feature is enabled, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before it goes live, and any personalized-advertising use of your device identifier on iOS will be gated behind Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt (your explicit choice).

4. Children's privacy

QuizPliz is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. U.S. state privacy rights (including California)

Because the App collects no personal information and creates no user profile, there is nothing to access, correct, delete, or opt out of selling. We do not sell or share personal information.

6. Data retention

On-device data persists only until you delete it or uninstall the App. We retain nothing on our side because we receive nothing.

7. Availability

The App is offered as a general-audience product and is not offered to users in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom.

8. Security

Since your data stays on your device, its security is protected by your device's own operating-system safeguards. Any network connection the App makes uses standard encrypted (HTTPS) transport.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves (for example, if the optional ads feature is enabled). Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date, and — where required — in the App Store privacy details.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy? Email alishersv.dev@outlook.com.

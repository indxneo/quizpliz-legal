QuizPliz

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 18, 2026 · Developer: Alisher Serikbayev

QuizPliz ("the App") is a fast, offline-first true/false trivia game. It does not require an account, a login, or any sign-up, and it does not ask you for any personal information such as your name, email, or contacts. Your gameplay stays on your device. The App does, however, include a small number of third-party Google services — analytics, crash reporting, and advertising — which are described below.

1. Information stored on your device

Your gameplay data — scores, progress and tier, daily-challenge streak, and your chosen language — is stored locally on your device only (in the App's on-device database). We do not receive it, and it is never transmitted to us. Deleting the App removes this data.

2. Information collected by third-party services

To keep the App free and reliable, we use the following services from Google. They may collect limited data as described here:

  • Google AdMob (advertising). The App offers an optional "watch an ad to continue" reward. To serve and measure these ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (the IDFA), approximate (coarse) location derived from your IP address, and ad-interaction data.
  • Firebase Analytics (usage analytics). Collects aggregate, app-usage events — such as a game starting or ending, clearing a block, watching a revive ad, completing the Daily Challenge, and sharing a score — together with standard device/app information and a pseudonymous app-instance identifier. It does not record which questions you answer, and it collects no name, email, or contacts.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (crash & diagnostics). If the App crashes, it sends diagnostic data — crash traces, device model, OS version, and app state — so we can fix the problem.

These services are operated by Google, which processes the data as described in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not sell your personal information for money.

3. Advertising & tracking choices (App Tracking Transparency)

The App shows rewarded ads through Google AdMob. On iOS, ads that use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) for personalization are shown only if you allow tracking through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

4. Children's privacy

QuizPliz is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the App is not configured for child-directed advertising.

5. U.S. state privacy rights (including California)

Some U.S. state privacy laws (such as the California Consumer Privacy Act) treat the use of advertising identifiers for targeted advertising as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. You can opt out of this personalized use: by declining the App Tracking Transparency prompt (or disabling it in Settings). We honor applicable opt-out preference signals. Because the App requires no account and collects no directly identifying information, there is no profile tied to your name to access or delete on our side.

6. Data retention

On-device data persists only until you delete it or uninstall the App. Data collected by the Google services above is retained and handled by Google under its own policies; we retain no separate copy.

7. Availability

The App is offered as a general-audience product and is not offered to users in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom.

8. Security

Your on-device data is protected by your device's own operating-system safeguards. All network connections the App makes — including ad and analytics requests — use standard encrypted (HTTPS) transport.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date and, where required, in the App Store privacy details.

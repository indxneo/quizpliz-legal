QuizPliz

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026 · Developer: Alisher Serikbayev

QuizPliz ("the App") is a fast, offline-first true/false trivia game for iPhone. It does not require an account, a login, or any sign-up, and it does not ask you for any personal information such as your name, email, or contacts. Your gameplay stays on your device. The App includes one third-party service, Google AdMob, for an optional rewarded ad. It is described below.

1. Information stored on your device

The App keeps your scores, your progress and tier in each category, your Daily Challenge streak, your settings (language, sound, vibration and Relaxed Mode), and a list of recently played cards so that questions don't repeat too soon. This is stored in a file on your device. It is included in your device backups if you use them, but it is never sent to us. Deleting the App removes it.

2. Information collected by third-party services

To keep the App free, it offers an optional "watch an ad to continue" reward, served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure these ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (the IDFA, only if you allow tracking; see section 3), approximate (coarse) location derived from your IP address, and ad-interaction and diagnostic data.

Google processes this data as described in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not sell your personal information for money.

The App contains no analytics or crash-reporting service. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple gives us aggregated usage statistics and crash reports, which do not identify you. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

3. Advertising and tracking choices (App Tracking Transparency)

Ads that use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) for personalization are shown only if you allow tracking through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

4. Sharing your score

If you tap Share, the App creates an image of your score card and a short message, and iOS lets you choose where to send them. They go only where you send them. If you save the image to Photos, the App asks for permission to add a photo; it cannot see your photo library.

5. Children's privacy

QuizPliz is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the App is not configured for child-directed advertising.

6. U.S. state privacy rights (including California)

Some U.S. state privacy laws (such as the California Consumer Privacy Act) treat the use of advertising identifiers for targeted advertising as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. You can opt out of this personalized use by declining the App Tracking Transparency prompt, or by turning tracking off in Settings. We honor applicable opt-out preference signals. Because the App requires no account and collects no directly identifying information, there is no profile tied to your name to access or delete on our side.

7. Data retention

On-device data stays until you delete the App. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained and handled by Google under its own policies; we keep no separate copy.

8. Availability

The App is offered as a general-audience product and is not offered to users in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom.

9. Security

Your on-device data is protected by your device's own operating-system safeguards. The only network connections the App makes are for ads, and they use standard encrypted (HTTPS) transport. Everything else works offline.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date and, where required, in the App Store privacy details.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy? Email alishersv.dev@outlook.com.

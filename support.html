Frequently asked

How do I play?
Each card makes a claim, and you have 5 seconds to answer. Swipe right for YES or left for NO, or tap the buttons. Faster answers score more points, and correct answers in a row build a combo. Regular games give you 2 lives; the Daily Challenge has none, so you always play all 25 cards.
What's the Daily Challenge?
25 cards from all six categories, the same for everyone each day. Finish it to grow your streak. If you miss a day, the streak starts over.
Can I play without the timer?
Yes. Turn on Relaxed Mode in Settings (the gear on the home screen). It turns on by itself while VoiceOver is running.
Does the app show ads or track me?
QuizPliz shows an optional "watch an ad to continue" rewarded ad from Google AdMob. Personalized ads use your advertising identifier only if you allow it in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, you'll still see ads, just non-personalized ones. See our Privacy Policy for details and how to change this.
What data do you collect?
None that reaches us. Your scores and progress stay on your device. The only third-party service in the app is Google AdMob, for the optional ad; what it collects is explained in our Privacy Policy.
Which languages are supported?
English, Spanish, and Russian. Change it in Settings (the gear on the home screen) under Language.
How do I delete my data?
There's no account. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone, including your scores. We don't keep a copy anywhere.
